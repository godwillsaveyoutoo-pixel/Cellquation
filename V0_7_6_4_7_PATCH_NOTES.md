# Cellquation Core v0.7.6.4.7 — Deep-Sea HUD Framing

Built directly on v0.7.6.4.6.

## Scope
HUD/framing only. The cell renderer, synapse renderer, aesthetic preset, gameplay logic, developer quality mode, and deep-sea PNG are unchanged.

## Changes
- replaced full-width hard HUD bands with inset rounded deep-sea instrument panels;
- top panel now has a subtle optical seam beneath the target cells;
- bottom controls are presented as a floating dock with softer framing;
- playfield gets a very thin inset frame so it connects visually to the new HUD without reducing the deep-sea scene;
- hint capsule is slightly narrower and visually matched to the HUD family;
- no backdrop blur or animated filter effects were added, preserving low-end performance.
