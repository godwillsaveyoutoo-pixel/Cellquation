# Cellquation v0.7.7a.10 — Nature Audio Loudness Normalization

The five user-supplied radio aporee field recordings were measured with EBU R128 integrated loudness and normalized for use as one in-game playlist.

**Processing target:** approximately -26 LUFS integrated, true-peak protected around -2 dBFS where limiting was required. All game copies are 48 kHz stereo MP3 at 128 kb/s. Original ID3 title / artist / album / year metadata was preserved.
